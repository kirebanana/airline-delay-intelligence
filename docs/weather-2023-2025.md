# Denver weather, 2023–2025

**Status:** Data published and restricted-reader acceptance passed, 2026-11-10.
**Requirements:** [Approved extension](specifications/den-weather-2023-2025.md).
**Source rules:** [GHCNh semantics](specifications/den-ghcnh-source-semantics.md).

The extension uses NOAA GHCNh station USW00003017 for Denver departures throughout
2023–2025. The earlier [January ISD pilot](weather.md) is retained as historical
evidence and an immutable release, not presented as the new full-period source.
No flight inputs, primary metric definitions, infrastructure or identities changed.

## Published evidence

Release `26e53444ad5a467fad7c930b3004b3da` contains all 36 accepted flight months:
22,562,477 schedules overall and 930,445 DEN departures, including 10,389 cancelled
and 2,609 diverted flights. Primary all-flight summary values exactly match the
retained prior release. Weather observation artifact: 120,708 rows, 83,671,259
bytes, content identity
`b09c9b959da0a56b81638be8855f577af685db8d6849097dc952d8a9a4f6a167`.

| Variable | Matched / all DEN schedules | Coverage |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Temperature | 924,873 / 930,445 | 99.40% |
| Wind speed | 924,795 / 930,445 | 99.39% |
| Visibility | 923,865 / 930,445 | 99.29% |

Coverage denominators retain cancellations, diversions and unmatched flights.
Frozen validation independently reconstructs source values and every flight's
scheduled anchor/backward match, rejects future matches/row multiplication, and
retains monthly and outcome-subgroup diagnostics. One fall-back clock (2023-11-05
01:19 local) has two valid UTC interpretations; its flight remains counted and
its weather is unavailable rather than guessed. The other 930,444 anchors resolve.

**Important limitation:** August 2025 flight-weighted coverage is approximately
90.1% and September 91.8%, below the overall result. Undocumented source999 records
remain rejected; source-specific quality rules were not relaxed to fill these gaps.
The approved >=95% gate applies to the entire scheduled population, not each month.
Use the displayed selected-population matching coverage when narrowing dates.
These comparisons describe associations, not weather-caused delays.
